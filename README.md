# 🌍 Convergence

> **Convergence** is a collaborative travel planning platform that transforms multiple individual itineraries into a single optimized group trip. By intelligently analyzing each traveler's preferences, budget, and schedule, Convergence recommends flights and accommodations that maximize satisfaction across the entire group.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🤝 **Collaborative Trip Planning** – Invite friends to contribute their preferred destinations, dates, budgets, and travel preferences.
- ✈️ **Flight Optimization** – Finds flight combinations that best satisfy each group member's constraints while minimizing overall travel inconvenience.
- 🏨 **Hotel Matching** – Recommends accommodations that balance location, price, amenities, and individual preferences.
- 🧠 **Preference Aggregation** – Combines everyone's itinerary suggestions into a unified travel plan using optimization algorithms.
- 💰 **Budget-Aware Recommendations** – Considers each traveler's budget when generating flights and hotels.
- 📅 **Schedule Alignment** – Identifies overlapping availability and proposes the most compatible travel window.
- 📊 **Trade-off Visualization** – Shows how recommendations impact each member's preferences, making compromises transparent.

---

## 🚀 Motivation

Planning trips with friends is surprisingly difficult.

Everyone has different:
- Travel dates
- Budgets
- Departure airports
- Hotel preferences
- Destinations
- Priorities

Convergence automates the negotiation process by finding travel options that produce the best overall outcome for the group instead of forcing someone to manually compare dozens of flights and hotels.

---

## 🛠️ How It Works

1. Each traveler submits:
   - Available travel dates
   - Departure airport
   - Budget
   - Destination preferences
   - Hotel preferences

2. Convergence aggregates all submissions.

3. An optimization engine evaluates thousands of possible combinations.

4. The platform scores each itinerary based on:
   - Individual satisfaction
   - Total cost
   - Travel time
   - Schedule compatibility
   - Accommodation quality

5. The highest-scoring group itinerary is recommended.
